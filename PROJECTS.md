Projects & Portfolio - GC Solar Projects
Executive & Business Overview
GC Solar Projects is a regional B2B solar EPC subcontractor and ground-execution partner headquartered in Rajahmundry, Andhra Pradesh. We specialize in end-to-end site execution, structural installation, electrical balance-of-system (BOS), and statutory liaisoning with state distribution utilities across coastal Andhra Pradesh

Strategic Positioning
- Primary Model: B2B subcontractor and execution partner for national EPC prime contractors, renewable asset developers, and industrial facilities
- Differentiators: On-ground execution muscle, specialized coastal corrosion-resistant mounting structures, rapid APDISCOM/APEPDCL interconnection liaisoning, and direct compliance handling under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana & PM-KUSUM frameworks
Utility Liaisoning & Regulatory Architecture
Interconnection Protocols (APEPDCL)
- Feasibility & Load Sanction: Direct processing with local Section Officer (SO) and Assistant Divisional Engineer (ADE) offices across Rajahmundry and Kakinada operational circles
- Transformer Capacity Verification: Distribution Transformer (DTR) solar hosting capacity validation (statutory ceiling: 80% of rated DTR capacity under APERC regulations)
- Net-Metering Infrastructure: Coordination for bidirectional Net Meter testing, procurement, calibration at APEPDCL Meter Testing Lab (MTL), and synchronized commissioning with local Line Staff
- CEIG Compliance: Chief Electrical Inspector to Government (CEIG) statutory statutory documentation and safety approvals for systems exceeding 10 kWp
