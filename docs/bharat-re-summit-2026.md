# Bharat RE Summit 2026 — Chiddu's field briefing

**Dates:** November 2–5, 2026 · **Venue:** Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi  
**Audience:** Chidvilas (“Chiddu”), GC Solar Projects  
**Purpose:** Turn supplier and developer conversations into qualified Andhra Pradesh execution, procurement, and O&M opportunities.

> Use as a meeting guide, not as a representation that any company has committed to GC Solar. Verify current contacts, capacities, product eligibility and commercial terms directly at the summit. The specific event dates and venue are reported by DD News: https://ddnews.gov.in/en/india-to-host-bharat-renewable-energy-summit-expo-2026-in-new-delhi-from-november-2-5-pralhad-joshi/

## GC Solar fit and desired outcomes

GC Solar positions itself as a B2B solar EPC subcontractor / ground-execution partner in coastal Andhra Pradesh. Its repo's PROJECTS.md describes work across structural installation, electrical BOS, utility liaisoning, PM-KUSUM, and coastal conditions; it also records one commissioned 3 kW Kovvur rooftop and other opportunities as pipeline, not completed work. Keep that distinction clear in every conversation.

At the summit, prioritize: (1) an authorized Andhra/Telangana channel or EPC contact; (2) written ALMM and BIS documentation applicable to the exact module model and manufacturing facility; (3) a live project lead with district, size, buyer, decision-maker and procurement timeline; and (4) a second meeting with a named owner and date. GC Solar can offer local surveys, installation execution, utility coordination, commissioning support and O&M scoping; do not promise capacity, delivery slots or certifications without confirmation.

## Six target conversations

Company names below are prospect targets from the request. For “Tech Solar” and “Sova Solar,” confirm the precise legal entity / exhibitor on the badge before discussing business; similarly confirm whether “Ish Solar” means Insolation Energy / INA Solar. Public scale data changes and can refer to planned, installed, rated, or ALMM-listed capacity—ask for the current auditable figure, not the headline.

### 1. Quant Solar

**Profile / scale:** Public search results returned an ambiguous “Quant Solar” identity and a claim related to a 200 MW module plant, but did not establish the legal entity, operating status, ALMM listing, or a reliable current capacity. Treat all scale and personnel as unverified until confirmed at the booth.

**Why GC Solar should engage:** Potential module supply relationship for commercial/industrial rooftops, agricultural pump projects, and future ground-mounted or floating-PV EPC packages. A local execution partner may be useful if they are entering the AP market; do not assume they have a floating-PV product or active AP projects.

**Press for:**
- What is the exact legal name, plant location, currently operating module capacity, and current quarterly utilization?
- Which exact module models are on the latest MNRE ALMM List-I, and can you share the listing entry, BIS registration and warranty terms?
- Who owns AP channel/EPC partnerships, and can GC Solar be introduced to that person?
- For coastal Andhra, what are the corrosion/salt-mist test reports, packaging/transport terms, minimum order and delivered-price basis?
- Do you have a floating-PV reference design or qualified system partner? Ask for reference project, anchoring/mooring and warranty boundaries; otherwise record “not offered.”

### 2. Tech Solar — confirm exhibitor identity

**Profile / scale:** “Tech Solar” did not resolve to one reliably identifiable Indian solar company in the available lookup. No named executive, manufacturing scale, or product eligibility is asserted here. Record the badge’s legal entity, website and booth number first.

**Why GC Solar should engage:** If the exhibitor is an EPC / project developer rather than a module supplier, explore Andhra subcontracting for site surveys, rooftop/ground execution, utility liaison and O&M. If it is a manufacturer, use the procurement checklist and verify exact product/model eligibility.

**Press for:**
- What is the full legal entity name and your role in the value chain: manufacturer, EPC, developer, or service provider?
- Which AP/Telangana projects are active or in tender, and who controls subcontractor onboarding?
- What work packages can be awarded to a local execution partner; what are qualification, safety, insurance, payment and retention terms?
- For PM-KUSUM opportunities, which component and state tenders are you currently pursuing, and who is the authorized tendering counterparty?
- Can we schedule a technical call with the AP regional/project lead within two weeks after the summit?

### 3. Sova Solar — confirm legal entity and current activity

**Profile / scale:** Search results did not establish current solar manufacturing operations, current capacity, or the correct “Sova Solar” entity. Do not conflate it with similarly named businesses. Verify company name, current activity and personnel before logging facts.

**Why GC Solar should engage:** Potential technology, module, project, or regional channel partner only if the exhibitor confirms a relevant active business line. Match any discussion to GC Solar’s coastal AP execution and utility interface strengths.

**Press for:**
- What is the exact legal entity and what solar products/services are commercially available today?
- If manufacturing: where is the plant, what is current operating nameplate/output, and which models have current ALMM/BIS documentation?
- If EPC/development: what AP pipeline has funded land, grid connectivity and procurement authority, and which package is open to subcontractors?

- Is there an O&M scope in AP? Request geography, MW under contract, response-time obligations, spares responsibility and measurable SLA.

### 4. Rayzon Solar

**Profile / scale:** Rayzon Solar’s own public pages identify it as a solar module manufacturer. Its 2024–25 annual report states 6 GW production capacity across Karanj and Sava facilities; newer public claims differ, so verify current operating and ALMM-listed capacity and exact model availability at the event. Source: https://rayzonsolar.com/img/investor/annual-report/Annual-Report-2024-25.pdf

**Why GC Solar should engage:** Strongest clearly evidenced module-procurement conversation among these targets; ask about supplying eligible modules for B2B rooftops and any EPC/developer partner introductions for Andhra. Keep module purchase separate from any EPC award.

**Press for:**
- What capacity is operating today by plant, and what is the current ALMM-listed capacity/model lineup? Please share dated list entries and BIS certificates.
- Can you quote delivered-to-site pricing, MOQ, payment/credit, dispatch lead time, damage replacement and serial-number traceability for coastal Andhra?
- What product warranty, performance warranty, salt-mist/corrosion qualification and claims process applies to the quoted model?
- Who handles AP channel development? Will you introduce GC Solar to an EPC or developer with near-term Andhra work?
- Do you support module-level O&M/claim resolution, or is that solely the buyer/EPC’s responsibility?

## Guardrails

- Do not present pipeline as awarded, quote a module as ALMM-listed without checking the exact model/facility on the current MNRE list, or imply a supplier is committed.
- Distinguish rated capacity from operating output and ALMM-listed capacity; note source and date for every figure.
- For floating PV, establish project developer, waterbody permission, grid connection, mooring/anchoring engineering, electrical design and O&M responsibility before proposing a role.
- For O&M, define scope, access, response time, spares, performance monitoring, safety and payment before quoting.
- No unverified personnel names are supplied. Ask for the current regional lead and capture the name/title from the person or official card.
